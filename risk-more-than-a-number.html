Why risk is more than a single number

Sylver Muhirwa

Draft

An average can hide a lot. Risk work means looking at the whole picture of what could happen, not only the typical outcome.

The average is not enough

Two situations can have the same average loss, yet one may swing far more from year to year. The spread matters as much as the middle.

Rare events carry weight

A small chance of a very large loss can matter more than many small ones. Good analysis looks at the tail, not just the centre.

Test the scenarios

Ask what happens if conditions change: a bad harvest, a spike in claims, a drop in income. This is what stress testing is for.

Explain the range

When you report a result, give the range and the assumptions behind it, so the reader can judge how far to rely on it.
