Understanding AI as a statistician

Sylver Muhirwa

Draft

Many ideas behind modern AI come from statistics. Seeing them that way makes AI less mysterious and easier to question.

Models are estimates

A model learns numbers from data, just as a regression line is estimated from data. Different data would give different numbers.

Training and testing

A model that fits its training data perfectly may have learned noise. That is why it is tested on data it has not seen.

Uncertainty still matters

A single prediction is not the full story. A range, or a probability, tells you how far to trust it.

What a statistician adds

  • Asking where the data came from
  • Checking who is missing from it
  • Measuring how wrong the model is, and how often
