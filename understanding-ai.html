Understanding AI

Sylver Muhirwa

What AI is

  • AI systems learn patterns from data instead of following hand-written rules
  • Machine learning is the part of AI that learns from examples
  • Deep learning uses neural networks with many layers
  • Large language models predict text from patterns learned in very large amounts of writing

How it connects to statistics

  • Many machine learning methods grow out of regression and classification
  • Training a model means estimating its parameters from data
  • Overfitting is the same problem as fitting noise instead of signal
  • Models are judged on data they have not seen before

Where AI goes wrong

  • Training data that is biased or unrepresentative
  • Confident answers that are wrong
  • Performance that drops when the real world changes
  • Predictions that are hard to explain

Questions I ask before trusting a model

  • What data was it trained on?
  • How was it tested, and on what?
  • What happens when it is wrong, and who is affected?
  • Can someone explain why it gave this answer?
