Things I Believe

Sylver Muhirwa

Data should serve people first

  • A model that doesn't support a decision is just an exercise
  • Start with the question, then look for the data
  • Explain results to the person who has to act on them
  • Local problems need local solutions

Every number carries uncertainty

  • Show the range, not just the estimate
  • Assumptions are part of the result
  • Be most careful when the answer looks too clean
  • Risk is more than a single number

Good data work is mostly preparation

  • Clean data beats clever algorithms
  • Pipelines you can trust matter more than dashboards
  • Document everything so someone else can repeat it
  • Simple models deserve a fair try first

Keep learning in the open

  • Write to understand
  • Small projects teach more than big plans
  • Share work before it feels perfect
  • Consistency compounds
