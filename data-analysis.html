Data Analysis

Sylver Muhirwa

The process

  • Define the question first
  • Collect the data and check where it came from
  • Clean and prepare it
  • Explore and summarize before modelling
  • Model or test the idea
  • Communicate what you found

Cleaning data

  • Count missing values and decide how to handle them
  • Remove duplicates
  • Make formats consistent: dates, units, spelling
  • Look at outliers before removing them
  • Always keep a copy of the raw data

Reading results honestly

  • Correlation is not causation
  • Report the uncertainty, not just the estimate
  • Check the sample size
  • Look at the whole distribution, not only the average

Communicating results

  • Lead with the answer
  • One chart, one message
  • State your assumptions
  • Write for the person who has to make the decision
