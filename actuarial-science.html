Actuarial Science

Sylver Muhirwa

What actuarial science is

  • Probability, statistics and finance applied to uncertain future events
  • Illness, death, accidents and crop failure are typical examples
  • The goal is to price risk fairly and set enough money aside to pay for it

Core topics

  • Probability and loss distributions
  • Survival models and life tables
  • Premium pricing
  • Reserving for future claims
  • Credibility theory
  • Risk measures and stress testing

Where it meets data science

  • Actuaries worked with data and prediction long before the term data science existed
  • Larger datasets and machine learning now answer the same questions
  • The discipline stays the same: state assumptions, quantify uncertainty, explain the result

Why it matters locally

  • Agricultural risk affects farmers' incomes directly
  • Affordable protection depends on well-priced risk
  • Better data makes fairer products possible
